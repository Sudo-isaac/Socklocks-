How It Works

Three Steps to Sock Freedom

It couldn't be simpler. No learning curve. No special instructions. Just clip and go.

1

Clip Your Pair Together

Slide both socks through the Sock Lock clip. The spring-grip holds them securely without stretching or damaging the fabric.

2

Wash & Dry as Normal

Toss the clipped pair straight into the washing machine and dryer. The Sock Lock stays on through the full laundry cycle.

3

Fold & Store Perfectly Matched

Pull out a perfectly matched pair every time. Remove the clip, store your socks, and you're done — no sorting required.
